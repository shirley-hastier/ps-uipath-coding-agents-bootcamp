# Reflection & Day 2 Preview

*Roughly 15-20 minutes to close Day 1. Don't skip this — the checkpoint here
is what Day 2 kicks off from.*

=== "Coach View"

    **Purpose**

    Convert "I built something" into "I know what I'm building next."
    Participants should leave Day 1 with their real use case identified,
    not just a completed lab exercise.

    **Key message**

    The lab you just built is a proof of the *loop*, not the deliverable.
    The next nine days are about applying that same loop to your actual
    business problem.

    **What to emphasize**

    - Non-determinism was a feature of today's lab, not a flaw — different
      participants built meaningfully different agents from similarly
      worded prompts. That's what "the agent is a collaborator, not a
      vending machine" looks like in practice.
    - By Day 2, every attendee is expected to have their use case selected
      (see the [executive tracking checkpoints](../program-overview.md#executive-tracking-checkpoints)).

    **Questions to ask participants**

    - "What surprised you about how your agent turned out?"
    - "What's the one business problem from your day-to-day work you want
      to bring into this bootcamp?"
    - "What part of today's setup or build gave you the most trouble, and
      what would you do differently next time?"

    **Recovery guidance**

    If a participant is still stuck on the Day 1 lab at this point, don't
    let it block the reflection — capture the blocker for office hours and
    move on. The goal of this checkpoint is forward planning, not 100%
    completion of the lab.

    **Transition into Day 2**

    "Tomorrow we go from 'I built one small agent' to 'here's how you design
    something production-shaped' — tools, integrations, human-in-the-loop,
    and how you'll know it's actually ready."

=== "Participant View"

    Use the questions above to reflect, and come prepared to Day 2 with a
    specific business problem in mind — not just "something to do with AI."

## Checkpoint: Day 2 readiness

- [ ] I have a working (however small) agent deployed from today's lab
- [ ] I can explain, in my own words, why I got the output/schema I got
- [ ] I have a specific business problem identified to bring into the rest
      of the bootcamp
