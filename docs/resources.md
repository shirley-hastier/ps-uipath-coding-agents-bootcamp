    employees/partners** and **external customer cohorts**. Resources below
    are labeled so a customer-facing coach knows what a participant can
    actually reach.

## Internal / partner resources

Require UiPath employee or partner access — **do not share these links
directly with external customer participants**; point them to an
equivalent public resource instead, or have your coach walk through the
content live.

- [UiPath Coding Agents Workshop](https://uipath-practice.github.io/CodingAgentsCourse/) — Partner Enablement lab guide

## Support

- Product feedback: use the `/uipath feedback` skill command from inside your
  coding agent, or ask your coach for the current escalation channel.
- Skill ownership follows the underlying UiPath product team (e.g., the IXP
  skill is owned by the IXP product team).

!!! note
    This program is delivered against a pre-GA capability. If something
    doesn't work as documented, that's expected — report it rather than
    working around it silently, so the product team can fix it for the next
    cohort.
