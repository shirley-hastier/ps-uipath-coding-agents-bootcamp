# Days 3–10: Office Hours & Checkpoints

!!! note "About this section"
    Unlike Day 1 and Day 2, these are **self-paced days**, not scripted ILT
    sessions — there's no delivery transcript to mine for a coach
    script. Each day below expands the master deck's one-line focus and
    checkpoint into a concrete self-paced exercise, grounded wherever
    possible in real mechanics and gotchas already confirmed from the Day
    1/Day 2 transcripts (cross-linked rather than repeated). Where a page
    goes beyond the deck's one-liner, it's your own use case driving the
    work, not a fixed script — treat the steps as a starting structure, not
    a rigid path.

## Daily coach rhythm

Three questions to ask at every office-hours check-in:

1. What changed since yesterday?
2. What is blocked?
3. What will you complete next?
