## A closing note on where this product is

This program teaches a **pre-GA** capability. Some things — MCP tool
creation, guardrails for conversational agents, tool-count limits — are
genuinely unresolved or still in development, not because you did
something wrong, but because the platform is actively evolving. Report
what doesn't work; that feedback is part of what improves it for the next
cohort.
